- Main Inventory list contains only cars not sold.
- Collapsible "Проданное Inventory" section contains all sold cars.

- Sold cars are ordered by sold date, newest first.
- If a sold car is selected, sold inventory opens automatically.

- newest expenses first
- dashboard ВЛОЖЕНО = repair expenses only
